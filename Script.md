# 🎬 Thép Lửa: Presentation Script

A script for showing the game to a customer, live or as a screen recording. It runs about
**3 minutes**. Shorter versions for a quick pitch or a social post are at the end.

> **Live link:** https://ngothanhnam96.github.io/portfolio-AI_HeavyArmor/
> (check before presenting)

---

## Before you start

- [ ] Open the live link in a fresh window, full screen, sound **on**.
- [ ] Clear saved scores (DevTools → Application → Local Storage, keys starting `heavyarmor:`).
- [ ] Have a phone ready with the same link, to show the touch controls.
- [ ] Optional: `?seed=7` so crates drop the same way as in your rehearsal.
- [ ] Optional: `?debug&play=karst` jumps to stage 3; `__ha.clear()` in the console skips to
      the boss if you are short of time.
- [ ] Choose **Dễ** (Easy) for the demo so you can talk while you play.

---

## Main script (≈ 3 min)

### Scene 1: The hook (0:00 – 0:20)

**On screen:** the title. Behind the menu the game is already playing itself.

**Say (EN):**
> "This is Thép Lửa, Heavy Armor: one tank against a sky full of aircraft. It runs in the
> browser, and everything you see, the tank, the planes, the landscape, the explosions, is
> drawn in code. No images, no sound files, no install. That's a live demo running behind the menu."

**Say (VI):**
> "Đây là Thép Lửa: một chiếc xe tăng chống lại cả bầu trời máy bay địch. Game chạy ngay trên
> trình duyệt, và mọi thứ bạn thấy, xe tăng, máy bay, phong cảnh, vụ nổ, đều được vẽ bằng code.
> Không ảnh, không file âm thanh, không cần cài đặt. Phía sau menu là bản demo đang tự chơi."

---

### Scene 2: Roll out (0:20 – 0:50)

**Action:** pick **Dễ**, press **▶ Chiến Dịch**. The banner "Đồng Lúa Bình Minh" sweeps in.

> "Four battlefields across Vietnam: rice terraces at dawn, white dunes, a karst bay at sunset
> and a neon city at night. Each one is a layered landscape that scrolls as the column advances."

**Action:** drive with **A / D**, aim with the mouse, hold the button. Point at the treads and
the antenna.

> "You drive with two keys and aim with the mouse. The treads roll, the barrel recoils, the
> antenna sways. Small details, but they make it feel solid."

---

### Scene 3: The fight (0:50 – 1:40)

**Action:** shoot down a wave of fighters; a wreck spirals down and explodes on the road.

> "Every plane you hit falls out of the sky trailing smoke and crashes on the road, leaving a
> scorch mark. Kills in a row build a combo."

**Action:** shoot a falling bomb before it lands; drive over a paratrooper.

> "Bombs and missiles can be shot down before they hit you. Paratroopers land and open fire,
> so you just run them over."

**Action:** the blue supply chopper drops a crate; drive under it.

> "A friendly chopper drops supplies: more barrels, rapid fire, homing missiles, a laser, flak,
> shields. They stack, so by the last stage the tank is a fireworks show."

---

### Scene 4: The boss (1:40 – 2:30)

**On screen:** hazard stripes and "CẢNH BÁO · BOSS XUẤT HIỆN"; the flying fortress descends.

> "Each stage ends with a boss. Its turrets and bomb bay protect the hull. You can see the bar
> turn blue: it's armoured. Knock the parts off first, then the hull takes full damage."

**Action:** destroy a turret, then press **X** for a nuke when the screen fills up.

> "And when it gets too busy, the nuke clears the whole sky."

**Action:** the boss explodes; the rank card and the workshop appear. Pick an upgrade.

> "Between stages you get a rank and pick one upgrade in the workshop, so every run plays a
> little differently."

---

### Scene 5: Phone and close (2:30 – 3:00)

**On screen:** the phone: ◀ ▶ buttons, drag to aim and fire, the ☢ button.

> "On a phone you drive with your left thumb and aim and fire by dragging with your right."

**Closing line:**
> "Thép Lửa shows how far a browser game can go with no downloads at all. The same engine can
> carry your brand, your characters or your campaign. Let's talk about the game you want to build."

## Selling points to have ready

| They ask | You answer |
|----------|-----------|
| "Who is it for?" | Brand campaigns, events and booths, game portals, gaming cafés, a studio portfolio. |
| "Can it use our characters?" | Yes. The tank, aircraft and bosses are code-drawn and listed as data; stages are short scripts. |
| "Does it need an app?" | No. Any modern browser on desktop or phone. No install, no login. |
| "Is it heavy?" | No dependencies, no build step, no image, audio or font files. |
| "How do you know it works?" | 37 automated tests, including an autopilot that clears the whole campaign; replays are exact from a seed. |
| "Different skill levels?" | Easy, Normal and Hard change armour, lives, enemy fire and boss health; endless mode for experts. |
| "Can people compete?" | A top-seven high-score table with initials, plus S–C ranks per stage. |
